What is AIPCore?

AIPCore is a decentralized node-based income protocol built on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC). It combines a powerful 2×N binary matrix system with an 18-tier upgrade path to create multiple streams of passive income — all governed and distributed by immutable smart contracts.

  • Free Entry: Join with $0 — no upfront investment required
  • 18 Upgrade Tiers: Scale your earnings from $5 to $327,680
  • 4 Income Streams: Referral, Matrix, Layer, and Global Pool
  • Auto-Upgrade Vault: Missed rewards fuel your next tier upgrade
  • DAO Governance: Community votes on protocol decisions
  • 100% Distribution: Every BNB paid goes to the community

Income Stream #2

Matrix Income — 70%

The largest income stream. AIPCore uses a 2×N Binary Forced Matrix — every new node is auto-positioned using BFS (Breadth-First Search). 70% of every payment flows to the matrix reward receiver at the corresponding tier level.

  • Binary Tree: 2 positions per level, creating 2L slots at Level L
  • Forced Placement: Positions filled automatically — no manual placement
  • Spillover: Higher tiers unlock deeper matrix layers for spillover income
  • Tier 18: Unlocks UNLIMITED matrix depth

Income Stream #3

Layer Income — 15%

When anyone in your 10-level deep sponsor chain makes a payment, you receive 1.5% per layer — totaling 15% distributed across 10 layers of upline sponsors.

  • Layers 1–5: Qualify by having a higher tier than the triggering payment
  • Layers 6–10: Must also have ≥ 2 direct referrals
  • Missed Rewards: If you don't qualify, the reward goes to your Upgrade Vault
  • 1.5% × 10 layers = 15% total layer income on every transaction
